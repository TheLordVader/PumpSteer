Confirmed failures
- Sparse accepted temperature changes miss the nominal 30/60/120 +5min windows.
- Early cancellation leaves an unused 125min blackout, despite one-pending limit.
- No bounded history explains actual issuances, cancellations and missing outcomes.

Correction
- Keep the existing frozen curves and maximum 125min lifetime. Evaluate at the
  actual later HA report timestamp, never at an interpolated nominal endpoint.
- Retain nominal 30/60/120 labels as minimum horizons; publish actual elapsed
  minutes/range explicitly. A late endpoint consumes only the greatest eligible
  horizon. Lower skipped horizons are unavailable, never extra successes/failures.
- Compare all frozen curves, including persistence, on the identical actual row.
  Summaries remain separated by model revision and nominal horizon. Reference-only
  rows are marked reference_only. Pairs do not imply independent episodes.
- Cancellation releases the obsolete blackout. At most one issuance remains open;
  a strictly newer source-report timestamp is required even across source changes.
- Existing source, mode, context, command, clock, gap and SAFE interruptions still
  invalidate the unfinished curve. Keepalive does not create an outcome.
- Retain at most32 diagnostic events and64KiB of their JSON, fixed-key saturating
  counters, at most96 outcome rows, frozen curve/revision/source provenance.
  Reuse existing HA Store and5min whole-file checkpoint cadence; flush on unload.
- Restore revalidates saved curves/outcomes, drops pending work, records its loss
  and prevents reissuing its old source report. Legacy fixed_v1 stays unchanged.

Limits and next gate
- Endpoint selection remains conditional on accepted numeric changes and valid
  continuity. Unchanged polling does not become new physical measurement evidence.
  Results are not representative of all operating time or all temperature reports.
- HA receipt time is not proven physical sampling time. Requested command remains
  an empirical coordinate, not verified delivered heat. External heat may confound
  a real trial and must be documented without inventing precise intervals.
- Longer horizons can still be unavailable when commands/mode/source quality
  change. No promise of a specified number of valid pairs in a short trial.
- Nothing here qualifies the RTM model or permits adaptive control.
- Candidate is suitable for a separately approved short passive verification trial
  using a version-matched, checked monitor/stop routine. Before start: confirm the
  installed candidate, TI enabled only for the trial, Adaptive Braking false,
  multiplier1.0 and all existing passive safety checks. Preserve bounded private
  snapshots of diagnostics and compare each real RTM/reference pair fairly.
- The earlier HA Core restart cause remains unproven and is a separate operational
  uncertainty. A repeat requires investigation; this patch does not claim to fix it.
