resume instructions are maintained in [THERMAL_INTELLIGENCE_HANDOFF.md](THERMAL_INTELLIGENCE_HANDOFF.md).

This is a development checkpoint, not model approval or demonstrated control
benefit. The integration only exposes diagnostics and passive recommendations.

real-world trial, upstream contact, PR or active assistance is authorized.

Historical validation receipts in `thermal-validation-20261018/` describe their
named older revision; they do not validate the current candidate. Private household
